Earn more strides

3 ways to pull ahead

Lunch Walk
2×
Steps between 12:30 – 3 PM count double
Every step in the lunch window earns twice the strides. A 15-min walk = ~1,500 steps = 3,000 strides. Go outside.
Squad Bonus
+200
Whole squad hits daily target → +200 each
When every member hits their daily target on the same day, everyone earns 200 bonus strides. Your squad is only as strong as its slowest walker.
Streak
↑↑↑
Daily bonus compounds — don't break the chain
Hit your target every day and your streak bonus keeps growing. Miss a day and it resets.
