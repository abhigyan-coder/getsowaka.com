3. Information We Collect

We collect only the information needed to deliver the App's core functionality.

3.1 Account Information

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Organisation or team identifier (provided by your Participating Organisation)
  • Profile photo (optional)

3.2 Health and Fitness Data

  • Daily step count, collected via iOS CoreMotion (CMPedometer) using your device's hardware motion coprocessor

Strydes does not access Apple HealthKit or the Apple Health app. Step data is read directly from device hardware via CMPedometer. We request access only to step count data and do not access location, heart rate, sleep, nutrition, or any other health category.

3.3 Device and Technical Information

  • Device model and iOS version
  • App version
  • Crash logs and basic diagnostic data

3.4 Usage Data

  • Features accessed within the App
  • Participation in challenges and leaderboards

3.5 What We Do Not Collect

  • Location data (precise or approximate)
  • Apple HealthKit or Apple Health app data
  • Contacts, photos, or media library content
  • Camera or microphone data
  • Financial or payment information
  • Biometric identifiers (Face ID, Touch ID)
  • Push notification content
  • Bluetooth data
  • Calendar data

4. App Permissions & Device Access

Strydes requests the following permissions on your iOS device. No other permissions are requested.

Permission Info.plist Key Purpose
Motion & Fitness NSMotionUsageDescription Reads your device's hardware motion coprocessor via CMPedometer to count your daily steps. You will be prompted to grant this on first launch. Revoke at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Motion & Fitness.
Background Fetch UIBackgroundModes: fetch Allows iOS to periodically wake the App (approximately every 20 minutes) to sync your step count to our secure backend. No user prompt required. Disable via Settings > General > Background App Refresh > Strydes.
Background Processing UIBackgroundModes: processing Used alongside Background Fetch via BGTaskScheduler to run the same background step-sync task. No additional data is collected through this mode.

Strydes does not request access to HealthKit, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, location, push notifications, Bluetooth, Face ID, Touch ID, or calendars.

5. Background Processing

To keep your daily step count accurate without requiring the App to be open, Strydes uses two iOS background modes:

  • Background Fetch (UIBackgroundModes: fetch) — iOS periodically wakes the App to read your current step count from CMPedometer and sync it to our secure backend.
  • Background Processing (UIBackgroundModes: processing) — BGTaskScheduler is used to schedule the same sync task when system conditions allow.

These background modes only read step count data and sync it to our backend. No audio, camera, location, contacts, or other sensor data is accessed in the background.

You can disable background refresh at any time in Settings > General > Background App Refresh > Strydes.

7. How We Share Your Information

Your information is shared only in the following limited circumstances:

7.1 With Your Participating Organisation

Step counts, name, and challenge participation data are shared with the organisation that has invited you to a challenge, for the purpose of administering the challenge and displaying leaderboards. Organisations are contractually required to use this data only for legitimate wellness and engagement purposes.

7.2 With Supabase (Backend Infrastructure)

We use Supabase to securely store user accounts, step data, and challenge information. Supabase hosts data on Amazon Web Services (AWS) and processes data on our behalf under its data processing terms.

7.3 With Other Service Providers

We may engage additional trusted third-party providers (such as crash reporting tools) strictly to operate and improve the App. Such providers process data only on our behalf and are bound by confidentiality and data protection obligations.

7.4 For Legal and Safety Reasons

We may disclose information when required by law, court order, or where necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of Sowaka, our users, or the public.

8. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on secure infrastructure provided by Supabase, hosted on Amazon Web Services (AWS) in Mumbai, India (ap-south-1). We apply industry-standard encryption in transit (TLS 1.2 or higher) and at rest. Access to personal data is restricted to authorised Sowaka personnel on a need-to-know basis.

While we implement reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, no system is entirely immune to compromise. In the event of a personal data breach affecting your information, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by applicable law.

9. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account remains active. If you delete your account, or your Participating Organisation removes you from a challenge programme, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required for legal, accounting, or dispute-resolution purposes.

Aggregated and anonymised data that cannot reasonably identify you may be retained beyond this period for analytics and product improvement.

10. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct any inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Delete your account and associated personal data
  • Withdraw Motion & Fitness permission at any time via Settings > Privacy & Security > Motion & Fitness
  • Disable background sync at any time via Settings > General > Background App Refresh > Strydes

  • Lodge a complaint with the relevant data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, email us at care@getsowaka.com. We will respond to verified requests within 30 days.
