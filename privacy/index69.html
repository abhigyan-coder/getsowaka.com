1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how The Sowaka Project Private Limited ("Sowaka", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, discloses, stores, and protects personal information of individuals ("you", "user") who access or use the Sowaka mobile application ("the App").

Sowaka is a business-to-business workplace wellness app that lets organizations ("Participating Organizations") host step-based fitness challenges for their employees, interns, and members.

By downloading, installing, or using Sowaka, you confirm that you have read and understood this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

3. Information We Collect

We collect only the information needed to deliver the App's core functionality.

3.1 Account Information

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Organization or team identifier (provided by your Participating Organization)
  • Profile photo (optional)

3.2 Health and Fitness Data

  • Daily step count, collected via your phone's hardware step-counter sensor and/or Android Health Connect (see Section 4)
  • Physical activity data, accessed through the ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION permission to read the hardware step sensor

We request access only to step count data. We do not access location, heart rate, sleep, nutrition, or any other health category.

3.3 Device and Technical Information

  • Device model and manufacturer
  • Operating system version
  • App version
  • Crash logs and basic diagnostic data

3.4 Usage Data

  • Features accessed within the App
  • Participation in challenges and leaderboards

3.5 What We Do Not Collect

  • Precise or background location
  • Contacts, photos, or media library content
  • Microphone or camera data
  • Financial or payment information
  • Biometric identifiers

4. App Permissions & Device Access

Sowaka requests the following permissions on your device:

Permission Why it's needed
Physical Activity
(ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION)		 Reads your phone's built-in hardware step-counter sensor (Android sensor type: TYPE_STEP_COUNTER) to count your daily steps. You will be asked to grant this permission when you first launch the App. You can revoke it at any time in your device Settings.
Health Connect
(health.READ_STEPS)		 Used as a fallback step source when the hardware sensor is unavailable. Sowaka reads only step data from Health Connect — no other health categories. You grant or revoke this separately through the Health Connect app on your device.
Notifications
(POST_NOTIFICATIONS — Android 13+)		 Displays an ongoing notification while step tracking is running in the background (e.g., "Tracking your steps — 4,200 today"). You will be prompted to allow this on Android 13 and above.
Internet Syncs your step count to our secure backend, fetches leaderboards, and loads your challenge and profile data.
Foreground Service & Foreground Service (Health) Allows the step-tracking service to run while the App is in the background (see Section 5 below). No additional data is collected through this permission.
Hardware sensor note: Sowaka uses your phone's built-in accelerometer-based step counter as its primary data source. This sensor data is processed on-device to produce a step count; raw accelerometer readings are not transmitted to our servers.

5. Background Processing & Notifications

To keep your daily step count accurate without requiring the App to be open, Sowaka runs a foreground service in the background. This service:

  • Continuously reads from the hardware step-counter sensor while your phone is in use or the App is backgrounded
  • Displays a persistent notification (e.g., "Tracking your steps — 4,200 today") so you always know tracking is active
  • Periodically syncs your step count to our secure backend

The service runs only while you are enrolled in an active challenge. You can stop it at any time by pausing or leaving the challenge within the App, or by revoking the Physical Activity permission in your device Settings.

No audio, camera, location, or other sensor data is accessed by the background service.

  • To provide step-tracking and challenge functionality
  • To display leaderboards, progress, and challenge outcomes within your organization
  • To authenticate your access to challenges hosted by your employer or member organization
  • To improve App performance, identify bugs, and develop new features
  • To communicate essential service updates, security notices, and changes to this Policy
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We do not use your data for advertising or behavioural profiling.

7. How We Share Your Information

Your information is shared only in the following limited circumstances:

7.1 With Your Participating Organization

Step counts, name, and challenge participation data are shared with the organization that has invited you to a challenge, for the purpose of administering the challenge and displaying leaderboards. Organizations are contractually required to use this data only for legitimate wellness and engagement purposes.

7.2 With Google Health Connect

Sowaka reads step data from Android Health Connect on your device, subject to your explicit permission. Health Connect is operated by Google LLC. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for how Health Connect handles data on your device.

7.3 With Supabase (Backend Infrastructure)

We use Supabase to securely store user accounts, step data, and challenge information. Supabase hosts data on Amazon Web Services (AWS) and processes data on our behalf under its data processing terms.

7.4 With Other Service Providers

We may engage additional trusted third-party providers (such as crash reporting tools) strictly to operate and improve the App. Such providers process data only on our behalf and are bound by confidentiality and data protection obligations.

7.5 For Legal and Safety Reasons

We may disclose information when required by law, court order, or where necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of Sowaka, our users, or the public.

8. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on secure infrastructure provided by Supabase, hosted on Amazon Web Services (AWS) in Mumbai, India (ap-south-1). We apply industry-standard encryption in transit (TLS 1.2 or higher) and at rest. Access to personal data is restricted to authorised Sowaka personnel on a need-to-know basis.

While we implement reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, no system is entirely immune to compromise. In the event of a personal data breach affecting your information, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by applicable law.

9. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account remains active. If you delete your account, or your Participating Organization removes you from a challenge programme, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required for legal, accounting, or dispute-resolution purposes.

Aggregated and anonymized data that cannot reasonably identify you may be retained beyond this period for analytics and product improvement.

10. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct any inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Delete your account and associated personal data
  • Withdraw consent for Physical Activity (ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION) permission at any time through your device Settings → Apps → Sowaka → Permissions
  • Withdraw consent for Health Connect access at any time through the Health Connect app on your device
  • Export your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Lodge a complaint with the relevant data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, email us at care@getsowaka.com. We will respond to verified requests within 30 days.

11. Eligibility & Minors

Sowaka is designed for employees, interns, and members of organizations that use our platform. By using the App, you confirm you are at least 16 years of age.

Users aged 16–17

If you are under 18, you may use Sowaka only with verifiable consent from a parent or legal guardian. The Participating Organization enrolling such a user is responsible for obtaining this consent prior to enrolment.

Users under 16

We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. If we become aware that such data has been collected, we will delete it promptly. Contact us at care@getsowaka.com if you believe a child under 16 has provided us with personal data.

12. Data Location & International Users

Your personal data is processed and stored in Mumbai, India (AWS region: ap-south-1). By using the App, you consent to your data being processed in India. If you access Sowaka from outside India, please note that India's data protection laws may differ from those of your country of residence.
