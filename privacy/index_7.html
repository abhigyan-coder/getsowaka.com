Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 11 June 2026  ·  Last Updated: 11 June 2026

Sowaka is a workplace step-challenge app. Your employer (the "Company") runs a challenge in which you track your daily steps, earn points called "strydes," appear on a leaderboard, and earn team bonuses. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, who can see it, and the choices you have.

If you have any questions, contact us at care@getsowaka.com.

1. Information we collect

Account details

  • Your name, email address, and username
  • The Company (and, where applicable, team) you belong to
  • Optional profile details you or your Company may add: profile photo, department, position, age, and gender

You provide these when your Company invites you, or when you sign in and complete your profile.

Activity data

  • Your daily step count and an hourly breakdown of your steps
  • These are read only from your phone's own built-in motion/step sensor — on Android the hardware step counter (the "Physical activity" permission), and on iOS the device pedometer ("Motion & Fitness"). We use this data solely to compute your steps and strydes.

Derived challenge data

  • Strydes, streaks, lunch/squad bonuses, team membership, rank, and badges, all generated from your activity.

Technical data

  • Basic request logs needed to operate and secure the service. Your sign-in tokens are stored only on your device.

2. What we do NOT collect

We do not collect or read:

  • Precise or background location
  • Contacts, photos, files, messages, microphone, or camera
  • Any health or medical data beyond your step count
  • A device identifier or advertising ID
  • Health Connect, Google Fit, Apple Health, or Apple Watch data — steps come only from your phone's own sensor, never from a shared health store

3. How we use your information

  • To run your Company's step challenge — counting strydes and showing your progress ring, leaderboard, team page, streaks, and bonuses
  • To keep you signed in and to sync your steps to your account
  • To operate, secure, and debug the service
  • To send essential service messages

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Who can see your data

  • Within your Company — your name, strydes, rank, and team standing are visible to other members of your Company as part of the leaderboard and team features. Your Company's administrator can choose to hide the leaderboard.
  • Service providers — we host our database on Supabase (which runs on Amazon Web Services in the Mumbai, India region) and host the application on Vercel. These providers process data only on our behalf, under their own security commitments.
  • Legal — we may disclose data if required to by law.

5. Storage & security

  • Data is stored on managed cloud infrastructure located in India.
  • Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS / TLS 1.2 or higher) and at rest.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on your device, never shared with other users.

6. Data retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account (see the next section), we permanently delete your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain limited records by law, in which case those records are kept only as long as legally required.

7. Your choices & rights

You can:

  • Access a copy of your data
  • Correct your data
  • Delete your account and all associated personal data

You can start an account deletion from within the app (Profile → "Delete account & data") or by emailing care@getsowaka.com from your account's email address. Full details are on our Data & Account Deletion page.

You can also revoke the motion/step permission at any time in your phone's settings (Android: "Physical activity"; iOS: "Motion & Fitness"). The app will then stop reading new steps.

8. Children

Sowaka is intended for working adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will revise the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, notify you in the app.

10. Contact

Questions or privacy requests: care@getsowaka.com
